Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Notes app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Baday Production (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Freemium service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".


Your Notes Stay on Your Device

The content of your notes, including all text, checklists, images, scanned documents and audio recordings, is stored locally on your device only. Note content is never transmitted to the Service Provider or to any third party. No service used by the Application has access to the content of your notes. The Service Provider has no server that stores your notes and no ability to read them.

The only exception is entirely under your control: if you choose to enable the optional Google Drive backup and sync feature, your notes are stored in your own Google Drive account and kept in sync across the devices on which you sign in with the same Google account. The Application uses two restricted Google Drive permissions: "drive.file", which limits it to the files it created itself - the backup archives in its own folder - and "drive.appdata", a hidden application-data space reserved for the Application that syncing uses. Neither permission lets the Application see the rest of your Drive. Your synced notes travel only between your device and your own Google Drive; they do not pass through any server of the Service Provider, and the Service Provider cannot access them. You can turn syncing off at any time in the Application's settings. Backups you export manually to local storage are ordinary files saved where you choose and are likewise never transmitted anywhere by the Application.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Premium version of the Application is completely ad-free, and no advertising related data is processed for Premium users. The rest of this section applies only to the free version of the Application.

The free version of the Application shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect and process device information such as the advertising identifier of your device, IP address, and coarse ad-interaction data. Advertising never involves the content of your notes: the ad SDK has no access to your notes, and nothing you write in the Application is used to select or target ads.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent via Google's consent form before any advertising data is processed (in accordance with the GDPR and the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). Depending on your choices, you may be shown personalized ads, non-personalized ads. You can review or change your choice at any time from the "Privacy options" entry in the Application's settings.

You can learn how Google uses advertising data at How Google uses information, and see the list of ad technology providers at AdMob ad partners. You can opt out of personalized advertising system-wide in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
